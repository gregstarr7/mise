Mise takes the stress out of dinner. Tell us your budget, dietary needs, and supermarket — we build a weekly meal plan and a shopping list that actually makes sense.

[Get support]({{ '/support.html' | relative_url }}){: .button}

## How it works

1. **Pick your shop** — Aldi, Lidl, Tesco, and other UK supermarkets.
2. **Set your preferences** — budget, dietary needs, kitchen equipment, and meal vibes.
3. **Get your plan** — five meals for the week, with recipes and ingredients grouped smartly.
4. **Shop once** — one consolidated list for everything you need.

## Why Mise

- **Less waste** — ingredients are reused across meals where it makes sense.
- **Realistic shopping** — lists built for how you actually buy at the supermarket.
- **Swap and reorder** — don't like a meal? Swap it for another from your week.
- **Built for busy weeks** — quick setup, clear recipes, no faff.
